# Prospective exact-T CN/US funnel protocol — frozen v1

Implementation: the naturally scheduled `scripts/run_cloud_daily_report.py` appends `prospective_observation` to the existing final JSON and projects its market/T summary into the existing HTML. The event rows remain only in the already permitted final JSON; no new production artifact or storage path is introduced. `PROSPECTIVE_EXACT_T_FUNNEL_V1`, `(market,T,symbol,setup)` and the existing first-confirmation lifecycle identity are immutable record keys. Duplicate account rows count once in the union. Conflicting causal account observations are marked `ACCOUNT_VARIANT_UNKNOWN` rather than arbitrarily counted or interrupting the production report; account-specific position sizing does not duplicate an event. Replaying the same natural report yields the same observation rows. The source run URL and Candidate seed source-as-of are retained when available.

The implementation projects only fields emitted by the causal daily result. Where the incumbent primary gate stops before target/RR evaluation, `all_fail_known` lists observed conditions and `all_fail_complete=false` with `ALL_FAIL_GEOMETRY_NOT_EVALUATED`; it does not claim a reconstructed all-fail list. QFQ and latest source timestamps unavailable per stock remain unknown; `DATA_OK` plus exact report T is a summary data gate, not fabricated provider evidence. Source logs from 9/22–23 cannot be used to backfill event rows.

## Question and observation unit

Test whether the sparse executable-opportunity observation persists after complete exact-T data coverage, and where attrition occurs in the unchanged strategy chain. The unit is one `(market, exchange session T, symbol, setup)` observation from the **union** of that day's formal pool and dynamic Candidate Included identities. Keep two membership flags and report mutually exclusive formal-only, dynamic-only and overlap buckets. A symbol in both buckets has one event identity and is counted once in the union. Dynamic-only remains `READ_ONLY_DISCOVERY`; it never becomes a formal proposal.

For each market/T, retain separate Seed, Stage A qualified, Included, Stage B requested/ready, formal-pool count, union analyzed, `DATA_OK`, `DATA_BLOCKED` and missing counts. Seed is the Candidate source population; formal names outside Included enter the union through their formal flag. A market/T with incomplete coverage remains in the denominator with missingness shown; do not silently drop it or divide formal decisions by Candidate Seed. Daily WATCH/ARMED are stock/state observations, not predecessors in a conserved event funnel. For opportunity comparisons, use every exact-T `DATA_OK` `(symbol, setup)` in the common union, including no-confirmation rows; show blocked rows separately and both rates per eligible observation and per market session.

## Causal capture

T is the exchange-calendar completed session, with source timestamp and source-as-of retained. Read only already emitted natural report outputs and permitted compact evidence at T close. For each observation record market, T, symbol, Setup, both provenance flags, Stage A/B inclusion/quality, latest/QFQ exact-T status, weekly/daily state, WATCH/ARMED, and whether this is the **first** causal CONFIRMED event. For a first confirmation retain stable lifecycle/event identity, T-known confirmation price, Decision, primary rejection, **all** applicable rejection conditions, T-known Entry Zone, nearest valid T1 price/source, structural invalidation, execution stop, gross T1 upside and R/R. Record `unknown` for unavailable fields rather than reconstructing them from counts. T+1 is a separate later observation of the exact next session's OPEN for an already allowed plan; no same-bar fill, T+2 substitution, future high/low/close or future Swing is allowed.

Keep a compact event row plus a market/T coverage summary and source artifact/run reference. Do not persist the entire Candidate raw OHLCV universe. Do not write Google Sheets, production strategy state, Paper ledger or broker orders; any necessary event export is read-only research evidence with no production policy effect. Reconcile first `CONFIRMED → Decision → ENTRY_ALLOWED → exact T+1 attempt → EXECUTED/skipped`, deduplicating repeated CONFIRMED snapshots. Retain all rejected, blocked and no-signal observations, and record every primary and overlapping reason without adding overlapping counts as unique events.

## Market boundary and falsification

CN and US are reported separately by exchange calendar, Setup and provenance bucket; no pooled rate hides a market difference. CN Candidate Seed is HS300 ∪ CSI500 with board/lot rules; US Seed is official IWB holdings with share-class normalization. Existing market-specific provider, latest verification and QFQ exact-T contracts remain unchanged. A day with `PARTIAL_DATA_QUALITY` is diagnostic, not evidence for a strategy opportunity-rate comparison. The data-quality-only explanation is falsified if complete exact-T coverage recovers while first confirmations and allowed Decisions remain sparse on the same declared denominator. A proposed formal/dynamic provenance effect is falsified if its event-rate difference vanishes when overlap, missingness, Setup and market/T are kept on the common denominator. This observation makes no profitability or execution-cost claim.
